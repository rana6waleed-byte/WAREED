WHY IT MATTERS

Why Donate Blood?

Blood donation is a simple act of kindness that supports patients, families and communities.

Support Patients

Blood is needed for surgeries, emergencies and the treatment of many illnesses.

Cannot Be Made

Blood cannot be produced artificially, so hospitals depend on generous donors.

Strengthen Community

Every donor becomes part of a network that helps families when they need it most.

A Small Time, A Big Gift

Donating takes only a short visit. Eligibility rules are set by health centers, so always ask them first.

SIMPLE STEPS

How It Works

WAREED connects people who want to donate with people who need blood in three steps.

1

Register as a Donor

Create your donor profile with your blood group, governorate and contact number.

2

Find a Donor or Request Blood

Search for a suitable donor by blood group and governorate, or submit a blood request.

3

Connect and Save a Life

Get in touch, arrange the donation and help someone in need.
